Good to know

Questions.

Which loaves count as a classic loaf?

Any loaf from our IDP Classics: Companio, Bagatta, Fougasse, Baguette, 100% Vollkorn, 100% Rye or the Organic “O”.

When do my treats reset?

Every Tuesday, when we open for the week. Monthly treats like the Flower Friday bouquet reset on the 1st. Anything unused doesn't carry over.

Can I share my membership?

Your card is personal and shows your name at the counter. The code on it changes every 30 seconds, so screenshots and old codes won't scan.

Does membership mean I can book a table?

We don't take bookings for anyone. It's first come, first served, every day we're open, members included.

How do I get my birthday treat?

Add your birthday in the app (Harbour Club and Île Royale). Your treat is waiting for you any day in your birthday month.
