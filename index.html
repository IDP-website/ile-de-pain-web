Before dawn

The fire goes on.

Long before the doors open, the bakers are at the wood-fired oven and the sourdough is going in.

08:00

Breakfast.

Doors open, first coffees and warm croissants. Turkish eggs, shakshuka meatballs and the Boatshed breakfast until 11:30.

03 / 05
11:30

Coffee & treats.

The half hour in between. Coffee, tea and something from the patisserie while the kitchen turns to lunch.

04 / 05
12:00

Long lunch.

Popeye salad, confit duck, sourdough flatbreads and a glass of something local. Lunch runs until 15:00.

05 / 05
15:00

Tea & last loaves.

Afternoon tea and pastries until 15:30. Don't leave empty-handed: we'll wrap any bread for you to take home.
